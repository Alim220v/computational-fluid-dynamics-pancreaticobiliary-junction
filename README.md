# Computational Fluid Dynamics in the Pancreaticobiliary Junction

This repository contains the correctly formatted version of my Master's thesis:

**“Computational Fluid Dynamics in the Pancreaticobiliary Junction”**

## Abstract

Pancreaticobiliary maljunction without biliary dilatation is associated with pancreaticobiliary reflux, a pathophysiological factor implicated in a wide range of diseases, including gallbladder cancer. This study uses computational fluid dynamics simulations to investigate the mechanisms and geometric features governing pancreaticobiliary reflux and to assess the potential impact of surgical interventions.

The results indicate that the **refilling phase** is the primary mechanism driving pancreaticobiliary reflux. Among the geometric parameters examined, the **cystic duct diameter** was identified as the most critical factor influencing reflux dynamics. Furthermore, the configuration of the **baffle system**, including baffle height ratio and number of baffles, significantly affected reflux behavior.

The study also highlights the potential therapeutic value of **cholecystectomy** and **endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP)** for managing pancreaticobiliary reflux in patients with pancreaticobiliary maljunction without biliary dilatation. These interventions may provide promising approaches for reducing reflux-related complications and potentially mitigating the progression of associated diseases.
